What was useful: the compact mainline arrangement, native structure-sequence lessons,
reusable bridge capabilities, 18 directed infrastructure paths, 16 station paths and
representative observed operation. These demonstrate working capabilities.

What remains unsatisfactory: the user-visible whole layout has fragmented paired
routes, a large outward hook and excessive spatial spread. There is no evidence here
that the entire footprint or height arrangement is necessary or optimal. The recorded
process did not consistently apply the principles already present in the handbook.

The procedure would expose those decisions earlier; it cannot prove that a smaller
replacement will build. That requires a new selected spatial plan and targeted native
trials, within a future construction brief. No replacement dimensions are invented here.
