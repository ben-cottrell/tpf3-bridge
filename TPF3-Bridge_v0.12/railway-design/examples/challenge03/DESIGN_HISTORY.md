## Arrangement and alternatives

Keep the A/B principal pair straight and level. C_UP leaves the southern rail
nearx200, sweeping toward a guide five units beside the flyover descent start,
then following its landing alignment in plan to the southern receiving lead. C_DOWN, described
outward for construction, leaves the northern rail nearx40, rises outside the
trunk to(220,25,18.5 world Z), crosses both through tracks at high level toward
(330,-80,18.5), then descends beside C_UP to its receiving lead atz3. The two C
routes converge to their final pair; temporary lateral/vertical separation around
the crossing is intentional and shown before fitting. The modest outward rise
provides space for height gain without changing the principal through alignment.
It is not an unplanned compensating loop added after other construction failed.

A diveunder would keep the crossing visually lower but require cutting below this
low-lying ground or elevating/reprofiling the trunk. A paired branch flyover would
add a second ramp/upper track without removing the single conflicting movement.
Select the single C_DOWN flyover for this first focused challenge. No fixed UK
radius/gradient is imposed; max_grade0.18 is a provisional proposal bound, while
the actual native engine decides acceptance. Drawn level-ended rises are roughly
13%, subject to actual fitted length. Do not flatten this into a radius-first task.
