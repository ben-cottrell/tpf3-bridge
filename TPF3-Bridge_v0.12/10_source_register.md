# Source register and evidence ledger

Register edition **0.6.0** · retained research checked **20 September 2026** · Project **TPF3-Bridge**

This register contains 63 source records, predominantly infrastructure-owner/operator publications, with one primary research paper. **A registered source is not necessarily a reviewed full document.** The review field identifies the actual scope. S023 could not be retrieved; standards entries S043–S047 are catalogue checks, not clause-level extraction.

A source date, asset edition, assessment period and retrieval date are different fields. URL directory dates are not publication dates. The v0.1 live-page checks and this v0.2 research were performed on 20 September 2026; retained records do not imply every page was re-fetched for v0.2. This does not make dated maps or project announcements current as-built records.

Each linked factual statement in this package refers to the source below. Engineering requirements and pattern proposals are original project design recommendations, not statements that a cited organisation endorses this bridge. Third-party PDFs and images are linked, not redistributed.

Version 0.6 adds no source records or newly reviewed clauses. Source-specific review fields and earlier-release access limits remain authoritative; a new package date is not a new retrieval.

## Claim reconciliation rules

Evidence is attached to claims, not only documents. Two sources can both be genuine while describing different dates, scopes or milestones. Store each claim with its scope and reject an unqualified merge.

| Conflict ID | Evidence | Treatment in v0.1 |
|---|---|---|
| EV-001 | Leeds station page and Platform 0 reports versus map omission | Accept the explicit 18-platform inventory for the stated page scope; retain the map as incomplete. |
| EV-002 | Leeds January and May 2021 completion wording | Preserve separate milestones; exact commissioning chronology remains unresolved. |
